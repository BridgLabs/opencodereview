### Changed

- The Reviews list shows every group at once, as collapsible sections in a fixed order: Needs you,
  AI reviewing, Completed, Drafts and skipped, Closed and merged. Before, the groups were tabs
  behind arrows, so most counts were hidden, and the open tab could jump to another group when a
  review finished. Needs you and AI reviewing start open; the browser remembers which sections you
  open or close. Searching or filtering opens every section with a match.
